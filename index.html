What is The Sift Dimension?

Mojang Studios Official Description
"A dimension that is beautiful, vibrant, and teeming with souls."
Unveiled during the live broadcast keynote at Minecraft Live 2026

Officially unveiled during Minecraft Live 2026, The Sift is described by Mojang Studios as a dimension that is "beautiful, vibrant, and teeming with souls."

Unlike past community mods like The Aether, The Sift is completely built by Mojang from the ground up to complement Minecraft's existing lore and cosmic hierarchy. Lead designer Jens "Jeb" Bergensten emphasized that while The End was rapidly shipped in 2012, The Sift represents a multi-year development effort designed to radically expand late-game exploration.

Canonical Mojang Lore: The Sift is an authentic 1st-party dimension natively woven into Minecraft's soul magic systems and the Illager storyline, establishing a definitive milestone for the franchise.

How to Play The Sift Early

First Playable Debut: Minecraft Dungeons II

Canonical Prequel Experience
Releases Sept 29, 2026

You do not need to wait until the 2027 vanilla release to experience The Sift. Mojang announced that The Sift is fundamentally integrated into Minecraft Dungeons II, releasing on September 29, 2026.

How Dungeons II connects to The Sift:
  • Rifts open across the Overworld following story campaign progression.
  • Showcases the Evoker's soul-harnessing powers and Illager lore.
  • Serves as the lore foundation before the dimension hits vanilla Java & Bedrock.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Click questions to expand

Q1 When does The Sift release for vanilla Minecraft?

Mojang has confirmed a 2027 target window for both Java and Bedrock Edition. Snapshot testing is expected in mid-2027.

Q2 How do you build a portal to The Sift?

Portal activation mechanics remain undisclosed. Teasers suggest ancient soul-infused blocks and rifts will be required.

Q3 Is The Sift related to The Aether mod?

No. The Aether is a community-made sky dimension mod. The Sift is 100% original official Mojang content.

Q4 Will The Sift be on consoles and mobile?

Yes, Bedrock Edition covers Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, iOS, and Android simultaneously.
