The Ancient City Portal Theory

The Dormant Monolith at Y: -52

Ever since Minecraft 1.19 introduced the Deep Dark, players noticed the striking centerpiece of the Ancient City: a massive 23×23 block rectangular frame composed entirely of Reinforced Deepslate. Unlike obsidian or end stone, Reinforced Deepslate is completely immune to survival mining, wither explosions, and piston displacement.

Frame Size

Blast Resistance

Dimensional Gateway

Built by the ancient civilization before the sculk parasite overran the underground.

Official Forensics

Mojang lead designers confirmed during developer roundtables that the Ancient City center was intentionally modeled after portal architecture, stating: "It is not an active doorway today, but ancient structures do not stand forever dormant."

Step-by-Step Portal Construction & Sizing

Universal Dimensional Geometry

Like Nether portals, Sift portals must form a closed upright rectangular or square perimeter. Corner blocks may be omitted or substituted with decorative stone to save rare resources.

1

Establish the Threshold Base

Place 2 to 5 frame blocks horizontally along the ground. If building a minimal 4×5 frame, lay 2 center frame blocks flanked by temporary scaffolding.

2

Erect the Vertical Jambs

Stack 3 vertical frame blocks on both left and right edges. Ensure both pillars are parallel and aligned on the exact same axis (North-South or East-West).

3

Bridge the Upper Arch

Place 2 frame blocks across the top opening to close the rectangular loop. Verify that the inner opening is completely unobstructed by torches or banners.

4

Soul Strike Ignition

Right-click the interior top-face of the bottom frame block with a Soul Fire striker or Echo Shard. The interior will transform into swirling bioluminescent teal mist.

Dimensional Ratio & Linkage Rules

The 1:4 Compression Math Explained

Minecraft's Nether utilizes a 1:8 ratio (1 block in Nether = 8 in Overworld). However, The Sift is calibrated with a 1:4 spatial ratio. This makes The Sift an ideal mid-range transit network: fast enough to cross thousands of blocks comfortably, but granular enough that nearby player bases won't accidentally cross-link their portals.

Coordinate Translation Formula
Overworld → The Sift
Sift_X = Overworld_X ÷ 4
Sift_Z = Overworld_Z ÷ 4
Sift_Y = Clamped(Y, 0, 256)

Example: Overworld (800, 64, -1200) links to Sift (200, 64, -300).

The Sift → Overworld
Overworld_X = Sift_X × 4
Overworld_Z = Sift_Z × 4
Overworld_Y = Preserved (±16)

Example: Walking 250 blocks in The Sift advances 1,000 blocks in Overworld.

Portal Search & Anti-Trapping Protocol

  • 128-Block Radial Search: When crossing dimensions, the engine scans within 128 horizontal blocks of the ideal target coordinate for an active gateway.
  • Height Independent Weighting: Vertical distance (Y-axis) is weighted at 1/2 of horizontal distance to prioritize existing grounded portals over dangerous mountain peaks.
  • Safe Air Cavity Generation: If no candidate portal exists within range, a new standard 4×5 obsidian/deepslate portal is generated inside a 3×4×3 air pocket with 4 blocks of obsidian floor.
  • Void Avoidance Protocol: Portals will never generate directly above The Sift's bottomless void abysses; instead, they snap to the nearest biome shelf.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you ignite the Ancient City portal in vanilla Minecraft right now? ▼

In current vanilla versions (1.19 through 1.21), the Reinforced Deepslate frame in Ancient Cities cannot be lit in survival mode. Mojang designed the 23×23 structure as a dormant portal foundation awaiting the official release of The Sift dimension update.

What is the coordinate ratio between The Sift and the Overworld? ▼

The Sift operates on a 1:4 coordinate compression ratio. Traveling 1 block horizontally in The Sift equals traversing 4 blocks in the Overworld, compared to the Nether's 1:8 ratio.

What frame blocks are required to construct a player-made Sift portal? ▼

Based on developer previews and lore materials, custom portals require Soul Crying Obsidian or Echo-Tuned Deepslate blocks, activated using a Soul Spark Ignition device or Echo Shard resonance.

How does portal linkage work between the Overworld and The Sift? ▼

When entering a portal, the game divides your Overworld coordinates by 4 (X and Z) and searches within a 128-block radius in The Sift for an existing active portal before generating a new link.

Can hostile mobs pass through The Sift portal? ▼

Yes, entities can traverse active Sift portals. Entities take 15 game ticks of portal contact to transfer dimensions, similar to Nether portal traversal rules.
