## Overview

Announced during **Minecraft Live 2026**, **The Sift** marks Minecraft's first official new dimension in more than 14 years, joining the Overworld, the Nether, and The End. Described by Mojang Studios as a realm that is *"beautiful, vibrant, and teeming with souls,"* The Sift introduces an entirely fresh ecosystem to the game.

## Confirmed Biomes

### 1. The Meadow
- A saturated, high-contrast landscape featuring glowing alien flora and reactive vegetation.
- Features shallow soul-water pools and ground-level exploration incentives.
- Native habitat for the newly teased bioluminescent frog mob.

### 2. The Carapace
- A stark, desert-like biome dominated by colossal fossilized skeletal structures.
- Shares thematic and visual ties with the Nether's Soul Sand Valley, expanding upon ancient soul lore.

## How to Play "The Sift" Early

Players do not have to wait until the 2027 vanilla update to experience The Sift. Mojang confirmed that the dimension is deeply woven into the campaign of **Minecraft Dungeons II** (releasing September 29, 2026). In *Dungeons II*, dimensional rifts open across the Overworld, allowing players to explore the dangerous environments of The Sift ahead of its survival debut.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is The Sift based on The Aether mod?**  
A: No. The Aether is a popular third-party sky dimension mod. The Sift is 100% original vanilla content created directly by Mojang Studios.

**Q: How do you build a portal to The Sift?**  
A: Official portal frames and ignition recipes have not yet been revealed. Teasers indicate ancient soul-infused blocks will be required.
