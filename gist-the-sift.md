# Minecraft The Sift: 4th Official Dimension Overview & Release Calendar

The Sift is the newly announced 4th official dimension in Minecraft (joining Overworld, Nether, and The End), unveiled during **Minecraft Live 2026**.

Official community tracker & interactive guide: [https://thesiftguide.com](https://thesiftguide.com)

## Quick Highlights

- **Status**: Official Vanilla Dimension #4 (first since The End in 2012)
- **Vanilla Launch**: Slated for 2027 (Java & Bedrock editions)
- **Early Access**: September 29, 2026 playable via *Minecraft Dungeons II* dimensional rifts
- **Confirmed Biomes**: *The Meadow* (bioluminescent flora, soul pools) and *The Carapace* (fossilized skeletal desert)
- **New Mobs**: Amphibious frog-like soul creature native to The Meadow
